0–9 or scroll wheel picks a block   C opens crafting   E opens/closes a nearby door   F eats meat   M mutes   K saves

Keep an eye on your health (hearts) and hunger (dots) in the top-left — hunger drains slowly over time, and once it runs out your health starts dropping too. Pigs, cows and chickens wander the world; hit one to harvest meat, then press F to eat it and refill your hunger. Staying well fed also slowly regenerates health.

Chop wood, then craft a table — that unlocks the axe, doors, roofing, pressure plates, and windows (crafted from sand — genuinely see-through, so you can watch for creepers without opening the door). Crafted tools (axe, fishing rod, hoe) appear as their own slots on the hotbar — just select one to equip it. Build a fully enclosed shelter with a door — at night, creepers come out and will sneak up and explode if they spot you in the open, but a sealed door keeps you safe, and they're gone again by morning. A pressure plate near a door will open it automatically when you step on it, then close it behind you once you step off. Whacking leaves has a chance to drop a seed — plant it on grass or dirt and a new tree grows there over time. Press K anytime to save your whole world to this browser so you can pick up where you left off. Craft one of everything at the table — axe, door, roof, plate, and window — and keep watch to the north afterward; something changes: a walled meadow opens up, with mountains on either side, a farmed field of crops to harvest, and a small village with wandering villagers by the water. Craft a fishing rod at the table too, then equip it from the hotbar and hold LMB while aiming at water. Growing crops need a hoe to harvest — equip it and chop a crop for food, with a chance of a seed you can replant. And once night falls out past the wall, watch out for skeletons — they close in and strike in melee, unlike the training zone's creepers, and crumble away again by sunrise. Every frame is a real ray march through a 3D grid of blocks — light, shadows and fog are all computed per pixel, no baked textures.
