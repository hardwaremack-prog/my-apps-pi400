MeshTap

Looking for MeshTap...

MeshTap shows everything going through your Meshtastic radio. It's a small program that has to be running first.

  1. Double-click the MeshTap icon on the Desktop (added by Set Up My Apps.sh), or find it in the Raspberry Pi menu under Internet.
  2. The first time, it downloads the Meshtastic library (needs internet once). Then plug in your radio and press Connect.

No icon? Open a Terminal and type:
bash ~/Desktop/"My Apps"/apps/meshtap/start-pi.sh --lan

This page keeps checking and jumps to MeshTap as soon as it's running.
