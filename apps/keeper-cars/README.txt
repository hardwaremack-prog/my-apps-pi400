KEEPER CARS
===========
A buyer's guide to 28 used cars that last a long time, are cheap to fix
and have parts everywhere (like the Chevy HHR). Each car has a purchase
price rating, 5- and 10-year cost of ownership with A-F grades, how many
miles it lasts, exact engine and transmission, best trim, part prices,
maintenance specs, snow and tow ratings, and a test-drive checklist.
Compare up to 3 cars, or check whether a listing's price is fair.

Open "Keeper Cars.html" in any web browser. Works offline.
