The 68K Mac Museum

Every Macintosh built on Motorola's 680x0 processors, from the 128K that started it to the PowerBook 190 that closed the line. Each exhibit lists the specs, what the machine meant, and what fails on it today and how to fix it. The museum stops at the 68040. The PowerPC wing is somebody else's problem.

Conservation Lab

Problems almost every 68K Mac shares. Read this before you plug in anything that has sat in a closet since the 1990s.

Sad Mac Codes
Class codeMeaningUsual cause
01 / 0001ROM checksum failedDirty or failed ROM, bad ROM SIMM contact, damaged trace
02 / 0002RAM data bus test failedBad SIMM, dirty socket, leaked-cap corrosion
03 / 0003RAM byte-write test failedBad SIMM or mismatched SIMMs in a bank
04 / 0004RAM mod-3 address test failedBad SIMM, wrong RAM-size resistors (Plus/SE)
05 / 0005RAM address uniqueness failedBad SIMM or address line damage
0F / 000FCPU exception during startupUsually software: corrupted System file or bad boot disk
68000 compacts show one six-digit code; the first two digits are the class. 68020 and later Macs show two eight-digit lines; the last four digits of the first line are the class.

Prices are US list prices at launch where known. "Last OS" is the last Apple system release that officially supported the machine. Workgroup Server and Performa models are listed under the Macs they were built from. Illustrations are drawn for this page and are not to scale. Benchmark scores come from Low End Mac's published Speedometer results. Collector values are a rough guide for working units and swing widely with condition; check recent sold listings before buying or selling.
