THE 68K MAC MUSEUM
==================
Every 68000-family Macintosh, from the 128K (1984) to the PowerBook 190 (1995):
specs, repair notes, upgrades, max RAM, overclocks, replacement RAM chips,
accessories and compatibility, a Sad Mac decoder, and a collection tracker.

Open "68K Mac Museum.html" in any web browser. Works offline.
Your collection list is saved in that browser.
