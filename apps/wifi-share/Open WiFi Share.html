WiFi Share

Shares the Pi's Wi-Fi internet through its Ethernet port and shows who's plugged in. It opens in its own window, so start it from the Desktop:

  1. Double-click the WiFi Share icon on the Desktop (it's added by Set Up My Apps.sh), or find it in the Raspberry Pi menu under Internet.
  2. Click Start sharing, then plug the other device into the Pi's Ethernet port.

No icon? Open a Terminal and type:
python3 ~/Desktop/"My Apps"/apps/wifi-share/wifi_share.py

Needs Raspberry Pi OS Bookworm or newer. More help is in README.txt in this folder.

Don't plug the Pi's Ethernet into your home router while sharing is on. Click Stop sharing first.
