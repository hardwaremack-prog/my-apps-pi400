Melody Accompaniment
Each track is an original synthesis, not a recording of any performance. Piano-native pieces (Für Elise, Ode to Joy, Moonlight Sonata, Rondo Alla Turca, Canon in C, Minuet in G, Nocturne, Brahms's Lullaby, Gymnopédie) now play through a sampled grand piano for a genuinely natural tone. The orchestral and string pieces use richer, layered synthesis and a shared room reverb instead of a single dry oscillator. All fourteen compositions are public domain; the note data is my own best-effort recreation of each piece's real structure, not a verified transcription of the printed score.
