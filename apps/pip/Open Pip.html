Pip

Your offline voice assistant. Plug in the headset, talk, and Pip listens, thinks and answers out loud, all on this Pi with no internet.

Checking…

Pip needs a one-time setup (about 1.5 to 3.5 GB, 10 to 20 minutes, 64-bit Raspberry Pi OS). Set Up My Apps.sh offers to do it, or in a Terminal: bash ~/Pip/install.sh. After that, the Pip icon on the Desktop or this button starts it, and this page jumps to it as soon as it's ready. On the Pi 400 it picks the slim, quicker setup by itself.
