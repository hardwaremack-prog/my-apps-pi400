1.4.2  (October 23, 2026)
- The speech recognizer download also shows a progress bar and retries now
  (it could sit silently on some Pis)

1.4.1  (October 23, 2026)
- Setup no longer sits silently on a slow or stuck download: it shows a progress bar,
  retries, and switches to IPv4 if the connection stalls (fixes hangs on some Pis)

1.4.0  (October 23, 2026)
- Runs on a Raspberry Pi 4 (4 GB) too: it spots the board and uses a slim setup
  (Llama 3.2 1B or Qwen 2.5 0.5B, the tiny Whisper ears, a quick voice, smaller memory)
- The installer makes room on small Pis by raising swap to 2 GB
- The web address is now shown at the top, with Copy buttons
- New "Share on Wi-Fi" switch at the top: use Pip from a phone or another computer,
  no config file editing; it remembers your choice

1.3.0  (October 22, 2026)
- Real documents: say "make a PDF" (or tap the new document button) and it writes the
  chat up as a clean PDF with a title and headings, saves it in Documents and opens it
- "Save the conversation" keeps the chat exactly as it happened, as a PDF
- More honest: it no longer claims to save, send or open files when it can't

1.2.1  (October 22, 2026)
- If Pip's files were updated while it was running, Pip now says to restart it
  instead of showing "not found"

1.2.0  (October 22, 2026)
- Easy updates: "Update now" in the gear menu, an "Update Pip" desktop icon, or ./update.sh
- Pip lets you know when a newer version is out
- Your models, voices, settings and chats are kept when updating

1.1.0  (October 22, 2026)
- Switching models now confirms on screen and out loud
- The top bar shows which brain is in use; each reply shows the model that answered
