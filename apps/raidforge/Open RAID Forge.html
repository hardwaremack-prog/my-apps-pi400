RAID Forge

Point-and-click RAID 0, 1, 5, 6 or 10. Boot from the SD card, plug in your drives, tick the boxes and it builds, formats and mounts the array, and brings it back after every reboot.

  • Pi 5: SATA or NVMe HAT (it can switch PCIe on for you) or USB 3 drives
  • Pi 500 / Pi 400: no PCIe, so use the blue USB 3 ports with powered drives or a multi-bay dock
  • Live rebuild progress, drive replacement, hot spares, scrubs, speed test and SMART health
Checking…

It opens in its own window. Or use the RAID Forge icon on the Desktop (menu: System Tools). The demo uses pretend drives and changes nothing. Building an array erases the drives you tick, and RAID is not a backup.

By hand: python3 ~/Desktop/"My Apps"/apps/raidforge/raidforge.py (add --demo to try it).
