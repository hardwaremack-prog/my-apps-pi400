BLUEPRINT WIKI
Your own wiki for projects, networks, processes and prototypes.
Pages with tables, checklists and code, pictures and PDFs dropped
straight in, and a drag-and-drop diagram maker for network maps and
flowcharts (it can even draw one from a few typed lines).

START IT
  Raspberry Pi: double-click the Blueprint Wiki icon on the Desktop
                (added by Set Up My Apps.sh), or run:  bash start.sh
  Windows:      double-click "Start Blueprint Wiki (Windows).bat"
                (needs Python from python.org)
  Then open http://localhost:8080 (the My Apps tile finds it for you).

OTHER DEVICES
  Phones and other computers on your Wi-Fi can open it too. The address
  is shown in Blueprint's Settings page.

YOUR WORK
  Everything is saved in the "data" folder next to server.py.
  Settings > Download backup makes one zip you can import on another Pi.
  More detail is in README.md.
