One click: make it fast

Turns on every safe tune-up below: full-speed CPU, compressed RAM, gentler SD card writing and fewer background services. Your files and apps aren't touched, and Put everything back undoes it all.

Tune-ups

Each one can be switched on or off by itself. Green means it's on.

Background services

Programs that start with the Pi and sit in memory. Turn off the ones you don't use.

Overclock

Runs the processor faster than the factory 1.8 GHz. The Pi 400's big built-in heatsink handles it well.

Takes effect after a restart. If the Pi ever won't start after an overclock: put the SD card in another computer, open config.txt and delete the lines between # >>> RPi400 Performance and # <<<.
