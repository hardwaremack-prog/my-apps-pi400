Reading the numbers

A token is roughly three quarters of a word. Generation speed on a Pi is limited mostly by memory bandwidth, so bigger models slow down in proportion to their file size.

Measured values come from Jeff Geerling's public AI benchmarks (Ollama, default Q4 quantization): Pi 5 8 GB 4.61, Pi 5 16 GB 4.88, Pi 500+ 5.55 and Pi 400 1.60 tokens/s on Llama 3.2 3B, and 1.20 on a 14B model. Everything else is estimated from those points using each board's memory bandwidth and CPU speed. Prompt reading is faster than generation; these are generation speeds.

Overclocking table

Common, well-tested starting points for every board. Each chip is different, so some boards go further and some won't manage these.

Before you start

  • Use the recommended power supply. Overclocks fail first on weak supplies.
  • Add the cooling listed. A board that hits its thermal limit throttles back and loses the gain.
  • Add the lines to /boot/firmware/config.txt (/boot/config.txt on older releases), save and reboot.
  • Raise one step at a time and stress-test for 10 minutes or more before going further.
  • If it won't boot, put the SD card in another computer and delete the lines you added.

Test and watch it

sudo apt install stress-ng
stress-ng --cpu 4 --timeout 10m &
watch -n 2 'vcgencmd measure_clock arm; vcgencmd measure_temp; vcgencmd get_throttled'

You want the clock to stay at your target, the temperature under about 80 °C, and get_throttled to read 0x0. Anything else means back off or cool it better.

Future wing

Rooms under construction, based on what Raspberry Pi's leadership said in a public Reddit AMA in May 2026.

Not before 2028

Raspberry Pi 6

CEO Eben Upton said the next flagship won't arrive before early 2028. Expect the same overall layout as the Pi 5 with faster CPU cores, more I/O and more memory bandwidth. No built-in NPU is planned.

Planned

Raspberry Pi Zero 3

The company confirmed a Zero 3 is coming, and warned it will cost more than the Zero line has in the past.

Ongoing · 2026

The memory squeeze

LPDDR4 prices rose about sevenfold over a year, so Raspberry Pi raised prices in December 2025, February 2026 and April 2026, and added a 3GB Pi 4 at $83.75 as a cheaper middle option.
