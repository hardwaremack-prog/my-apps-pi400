It's okay to pass

Some buyers are resellers. They need your item cheap so they can sell it for more. That's their business plan, not your problem.

Someone who can't pay what it's worth isn't your buyer. The right one usually shows up within a week or two.

A real buyer who's polite, shows up, and asks good questions? Giving them a small discount is a nice way to close the deal.

Stay safe

  • Meet in a busy public spot. Many police stations have a safe exchange zone.
  • Bring someone with you for big or expensive items.
  • Take cash, or confirm the payment arrived before handing over the item.
  • Never share a verification code sent to your phone. That's always a scam.
  • Never accept a check for more than the price, or send money back.
