Your prices

Pricing tips

  • Too cheap makes people wonder why. A fair price says you know what you're doing.
  • Give the price before you start, in writing. No surprises for either of you.
  • Charge a minimum for small jobs. Your drive and setup time count.
  • After 5 good reviews, raise your price a little. You've earned it.
  • Offer regulars a small deal, like every 4th mow at half price. It keeps them coming back.
